## Coverage caveats

- This file indexes only the 22 bosses present in one SQL boss-kit migration; absence from this index does **not** establish absence from the wider repository or the game.
- Many other dungeon bosses have encounter/wing mappings but no row in this specific kit. Do not equate encounter coverage with mechanics coverage.
- These 64 records include 61 timer events, 2 health triggers and 1 pull trigger; do not turn non-timer events into cooldowns.
- The custom dungeons and Karazhan Crypts remain high-priority evidence gaps.
- Future work: match every scoped dungeon encounter against **all** saved mechanics/script files, not only this SQL dataset, and mark source confidence per boss.
- Loot is excluded; AtlasLoot-CoA v8.1.6 remains the accepted loot reference.
