# Local research artifacts — 2026-10-31

## Evidence status
These files retain their own audit notes and provenance labels. Many datasets are partial or source-derived candidates, not verified live Conquest Reborn facts. In particular, source presence does not establish live quest availability, encounter timing, or trainer acquisition. Review each folder's audit before using records as authoritative.
