What is preserved?

This is an actively reconstructed historical reference. Source catalogs and metadata are available now; complete original log reports, phase-specific BiS sets and validated tier rankings have not yet been recovered. Historical, third-party, and derived material are labeled separately. We respect source licensing and the CoA Logs restriction on bulk redistribution.

For original source citations and evidence status, visit the research catalog. Conquest Reborn gameplay may differ from historical CoA.
