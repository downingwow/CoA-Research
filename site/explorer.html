DBM encounter reconstruction

Boss mechanics should be confirmed from repeated logs before assigning timers. Use cast-start/success, aura, death and damage events to identify abilities, targets and encounter transitions.

Workflow: capture or open a log → review cast event frequencies and timestamps → associate source GUID with boss/NPC ID → compare multiple pulls → derive cast interval ranges → test in Conquest Reborn → publish DBM module with provenance.
